Who I am & how I work

Fuad Al Maimun

LocationDhaka, Bangladesh
FocusHTML · CSS · JavaScript
Currently learningReact & Node.js
StatusOpen to work

I'm a frontend developer who genuinely enjoys the craft of turning a design into a website that feels solid — the kind that loads fast, holds up on a small screen, and doesn't break when someone clicks the wrong thing. That attention to detail is what I try to bring to every project.

My core toolkit is HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript. I build responsive layouts with Flexbox and Grid, keep my code organized in Git, and I'm currently expanding into React, REST APIs, and Node.js so I can take on more complete, end-to-end projects over time. I don't claim to know everything — I'd rather be upfront about where I am and keep improving in public.

What drives me is the problem-solving side of the work: figuring out why a layout breaks at a certain width, why a page feels slow, or how to make an interface easier to use. I care about accessibility and performance not as buzzwords, but because they're what separates a page that looks good from one that actually works for everyone.
